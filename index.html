01 — Profile

About Me

I am a computational astrophysicist from Alem Ketema, Ethiopia, currently pursuing a Ph.D. at Ariel University, Israel, under the supervision of Prof. Amit Kashi. My research bridges 3D AMR hydrodynamic simulations and observational X-ray data to decode the violent interactions within massive binary stellar systems.

With an M.Sc. from Entoto Observatory & Research Center (CGPA 3.98/4.00, Addis Ababa University) and a B.Sc. in Applied Physics from Debre Berhan University, I served as Head of the Physics Department at Dilla University, mentoring over 100 students and driving curriculum innovation in Ethiopia.

I am a strong advocate for open science and STEM outreach. As ESSS Branch Chairperson at Dilla University (2020–2025), I organised national space science events and public astronomy lectures — because the universe belongs to everyone.

04 — Science

Research Interests

Colliding-Wind Binaries

3D AMR hydrodynamic simulations of wind-wind collision regions; radiative cooling, NTSI, Kelvin-Helmholtz instabilities, and synthetic X-ray spectra.

Radiative Transfer

Modelling photon transport through dense stellar winds using RADMC-3D and TORUS; infrared and X-ray synthetic observables for comparison with XMM-Newton and Chandra.

AGN & Accretion

Alternative accretion-rate diagnostics via X-ray/optical flux ratios; cross-matching 4XMM-DR14 with SDSS and Galaxy Zoo morphological classifications.

Dark Energy & Modified Gravity

Exploring f(R,T,S) extended gravity models, equation-of-state constraints, and large-scale structure formation in a dark-energy-dominated cosmos.
