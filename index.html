01. About Me

I am a passionate astrophysicist from Ethiopia, currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Computational Astrophysics at Ariel University, Israel. My research journey began in the highlands of Shewa and has taken me across continents, driven by an unwavering curiosity about the cosmos.

With a strong foundation in both observational and theoretical astrophysics, I specialize in radiative transfer modeling, binary star interactions, and galaxy evolution. My work combines Python-based simulations with multi-wavelength observational data to unravel the physics of some of the universe's most energetic phenomena.

Beyond research, I am committed to education and mentorship, having taught physics and astrophysics to hundreds of students and led departmental initiatives at Dilla University. I believe in making astrophysics accessible and inspiring the next generation of African scientists.

04. Research Interests

Galaxy Formation & Evolution

Investigating processes driving galaxy growth, dark matter roles, star formation, and feedback mechanisms.

Black Hole Physics

Studying formation, accretion, mergers, and AGN feedback in galaxy evolution.

Massive Binaries

Exploring formation, evolution, and wind-collision physics in massive binary systems.

Accretion & Feedback

Modeling accretion mechanisms and feedback from disks, supernovae, and AGN.

Dark Energy & Modified Gravity

Studying f(R,T,S) models and their impact on cosmic expansion and structure formation.

Exoplanets & Planet Formation

Researching planetary formation, evolution, and detection methods.
